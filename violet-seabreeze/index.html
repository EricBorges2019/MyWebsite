Why this page exists

I found Violet Seabreeze as a single HTML file sitting on a hard drive, with no readme, no folder of notes, and no idea how it got there. It turned out to be a finished, polished piece of interactive fiction that somebody clearly put months into — and the only trace of who they are is a first-person dedications screen signed Shiza.

No email. No handle. No link to an itch.io page or a devlog. Episode 3 was “in development” when this build was made, and I have no way of knowing whether it ever came out.

So I’ve put the file up here, exactly as I found it, along with every identifying detail I could pull out of it. If you are Shiza, or you know them, or any of this simply looks familiar — please get in touch.

The credits, verbatim

These are the exact words from the game’s Dedications screen:

I, Shiza, programmed and wrote this story. That much is true. However, this was not a one-person project. The people in this section have helped me, knowingly or otherwise, in a number of ways. Thus, I owe my thanks to: Pokson: For being the greatest help anyone can ask for. Studio Élan inspired me to get started on this project, but Pokson's support is the reason I kept going with this! Amaya: For being the other member of the Bavarian Illuminati and helping playtest the game! Studio Élan: Heart of the Woods was what inspired me to turn what would've otherwise been a static story forever stuck in the depths of the failing ASUS I wrote this on into a Twine game and publish it!

And from the end-of-episode screen:

You've reached the end of the road for the current build of Violet Seabreeze! The game is planned to be an episodic release, so I hope you don't mind seeing this screen often! What you've just completed is episode two, On That Day, When the Star Became Music.... Do rest assured, this is not the end of the story [...] and episode 3 is in fact on the way! As always, I'd like to thank you for sticking with me this far- your support means a lot to me! Shiza

Three names to go on: Shiza (writer and programmer), Pokson, and Amaya — the latter two described, jointly, as “the Bavarian Illuminati.” That phrasing suggests a small, close friend group rather than a studio.

Structure of this episode:

  • Prologue — The Girl in the Tide Pool
  • Chapter One — Recovery of an Injured Sea Spirit
  • Chapter Two — Painting the Town Blue
  • Chapter Three — Separation
  • Act Four — Violent Spear GUNGNIR (title card only; this build ends here)

The story itself: Carina, a fisherwoman on a cliff-ringed island, hauls her catch in ahead of a storm and finds an injured girl in a tide pool. The girl gives her name as Soulinari, is not human, and needs somewhere to hide. It is a slow, warm, sapphic romance with a fair amount of choice-tracking under the hood — the game quietly remembers small things, like what you bought her to drink, and pays them off later. The setting is Italian-flavoured: a Forte di San Giorgio, a manufacturer “based in Genova.”

The biggest clue: Symphogear

Every single passage in the file is named after a lyric or a transformation incantation from Senki Zesshou Symphogear — Balwisyall Nescell Gungnir Tron, Killiter Ichaival Tron, Seilien Coffin Airgetlamh Tron, and dozens of lines from the songs. Players never see these names; they’re internal. Shiza did it for themselves.

The episode roadmap goes further — each planned episode is named after a Symphogear season subtitle:

  • Meteoroid-Falling, Burning, and Disappear, Then... — Episode 1, marked Complete
  • On That Day, When the Star Became Music... — Episode 2, Complete (this file)
  • Believe in Justice and Hold a Determination to Fist — Episode 3, In Development
  • By Shedding Many Tears, the Reality You Face is... — TBA
  • Create a History, With the Light God Could Not Know. — TBA

So: a Symphogear fan, deep enough in it to structure a five-part release around the series, who was also inspired by Studio Élan’s Heart of the Woods. That is a fairly specific person to be looking for.

If Episode 1 exists somewhere — and the roadmap says it does — it would have been released publicly under this same name. I’d love a copy of that too.
